Privacy Policy

Orinzent Calculator • Last updated: January 2026

1. Overview

This Calculator app is built with a fundamental commitment to user privacy. We do not require account creation, and most core features are designed to work entirely offline. Our philosophy is simple: we do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or contacts.

2. Data Collection

We do not collect or store personal data. All calculations and inputs remain strictly on your device.

AI Calculator: If you use the AI Calculator feature, the text you enter may be processed temporarily to generate responses. This data is transient, processed in real-time, and is never stored or linked to your identity.

3. Data Storage

Some data may be stored locally on your device for core application functionality. This data is never transmitted to any external server:

  • Calculation history
  • App preferences (theme, number formatting, default units)

4. Data Retention

All local data remains exclusively on your device until you manually clear it or uninstall the app. We do not retain any user data on external servers.

5. Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by clearing app data in your device settings or uninstalling the app. This permanently removes all locally stored calculation records.

6. Third-Party Services

This app does not use third-party analytics, advertisements, or tracking services. If AI functionality is utilized, it may rely on local or external processing models, but no personally identifiable information is collected or stored.

7. Permissions

The app does not request sensitive permissions such as location, contacts, microphones, or camera access.

8. Children’s Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect personal data from children and is completely safe for general audiences of all ages.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected directly on this page.
